About us

A dedicated tower erection contractor, built for tier-1 EPC delivery.

Singhinfra is a specialist tower erection company operating across India's power transmission sector. We take up dedicated erection packages from tier-1 EPC contractors and deliver them with disciplined site planning, trained rigging crews, and safety-first execution.

Since 2022, we have built our reputation the way this industry demands: dependable erection execution, precise structural assembly, and disciplined field delivery — package after package, site after site. Foundation work is already in place before mobilization, and conductor stringing is performed by the designated stringing contractor. Headquartered in Khagaria, Bihar, our crews mobilize to transmission corridors across the region.
