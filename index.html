What we do on a tower package

We do the steel erection. Foundations and conductor stringing belong to other contractors, so you know exactly where our work starts and stops.

  1. Mobilization and site readiness

    Crew planning, survey coordination and site preparation against the engineering drawings and schedule.

  2. Tower assembly and erection

    Assembly sequencing and erection of lattice towers along the transmission corridor.

  3. Rigging, alignment and final adjustment

    Heavy-lift rigging, positioning and final structural alignment with controlled checkpoints.

  4. Safety and quality checks to handover

    PPE enforcement, risk control and quality checks from the first day on site to handover.

Not in our scope

Foundation work. The main contractor completes it before we mobilize.

Conductor stringing. The designated stringing contractor does it.

Safety on our sites

Zero-harm policy

Work does not start until it can be done safely. This applies on every site.

PPE for every worker

We enforce PPE use and safe work methods on all sites, including work near live lines.

CEA-aligned practice

Our methods follow CEA safety guidance and the extra requirements of each EPC contractor.
