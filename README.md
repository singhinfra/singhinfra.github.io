Add your own tower photo (recommended)
1. Save a landscape photo of a tower under erection as images/hero.jpg (at least 1600 px wide, under 400 KB).
2. Put the tower on the right half of the photo; the headline sits on the left.
3. Upload it to the images folder. It replaces the drawing automatically.

Before publishing
1. In index.html find: var WHATSAPP = '';  and put your number between the quotes with country code, no + or spaces, e.g. '919876543210'.
2. Upload index.html and the images folder.
